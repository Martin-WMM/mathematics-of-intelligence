\chapter{Diffusion, Scores, and Flows}
\label{ch:diffusion}

Iterative generation corrupts data with noise and learns to reverse the corruption. Scores, SDEs, and deterministic flows are different coordinates on the same idea: transport a simple distribution onto a data distribution.

The conceptual graph is more important than the product name:
\[
\text{noise}\ \rightarrow\ \text{score}\ \rightarrow\ \text{SDE/ODE}\ \rightarrow\ \text{sampler}.
\]
