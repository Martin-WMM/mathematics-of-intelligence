\chapter{Representations in Modern AI}
\label{ch:representations-in-ai}

Contemporary models learn the encoding instead of hand-crafting it. Embeddings, autoencoders, and contrastive objectives all choose a space so that later linear operations become meaningful.

Multimodal systems go further: they align images, text, and actions in a shared space. Whether that space is universal or always task-relative remains an open question of this part.
