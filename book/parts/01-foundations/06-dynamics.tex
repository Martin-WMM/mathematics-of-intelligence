\chapter{Dynamics and Iteration}
\label{ch:dynamics}

Each update is a map $\theta_{t+1} = T(\theta_t, \xi_t)$ driven by stochastic increments. Stability, noise, and step-size schedules are properties of that dynamical system.

The same language later describes learning algorithms, denoising flows, and closed-loop control. The chapter treats iteration as an object, not as a decoration on ``running an optimiser''.
